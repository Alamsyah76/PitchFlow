# CODING_RULES.md

## Core Mission

Build a Semantic Content Intelligence Platform.

Do not build a keyword engine.

Do not build a caption spinner.

Do not build a PDF summarizer.

---

# Development Priority

Current Priority:

SCI Step 1
→ SCI Step 2
→ SCI Step 3
→ SCI Step 4

Only after SCI validation reaches >= 85%:

Corpus RAG

Only after Corpus RAG is stable:

Agentic AI

Only after Agentic AI is stable:

MCP-like integration

# Do Not Do

Never prioritize:

- dominant_terms
- keyword overlap scoring
- noun stitching
- token assembly
- keyword density logic
- keyword-first generation

---

# Retrieval Rules

Retrieval is evidence.

Retrieval is not understanding.

Retrieved chunks must be interpreted before content generation.

---

# Validation Rules

Browser validation is mandatory.

Success metrics:

- semantic accuracy
- business relevance
- narrative quality
- cross-domain stability

Target:

>= 85%

---

# Corpus RAG Rules

Do not upload raw PDF directly into RAG.

Preferred flow:

PDF
→ OCR
→ Markdown/Text
→ Clean Corpus
→ Metadata
→ Chunking
→ Knowledge Extraction
→ Ontology
→ RAG

---

# Agentic AI Rules

Do not implement Agentic AI before SCI is validated.

Agentic AI depends on SCI.

SCI comes first.

---

# Golden Rule

Meaning First.

Business Understanding First.

Narrative Intelligence First.

Keywords Last.
